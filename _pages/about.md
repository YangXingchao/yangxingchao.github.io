My name is Xingchao Yang and I work as a senior research scientist at [CyberAgent AI Lab](https://cyberagent.ai/ailab/), in the Generative Media team of the Entertainment Division. Our team studies video and image creation and editing, with applications to advertising, anime, and games. I obtained my PhD from the [Computational Geometry and Graphics Lab](http://www.cgg.cs.tsukuba.ac.jp/) at the University of Tsukuba, where I was advised by Associate Professor [Yoshihiro Kanamori](http://kanamori.cs.tsukuba.ac.jp/index.html). I earned my master's degree from the [Yamaguchi Lab](https://www.graco.c.u-tokyo.ac.jp/yama-lab/index.php) at the University of Tokyo.

My own research interests span deep learning, computer vision, and computer graphics, and beyond research I also have a strong interest in [cloud engineering](https://www.credential.net/1b932925-990a-4ccf-81bc-e3316fbf3e98) and [game development](https://www.credly.com/badges/ea85c335-617f-41f3-ab8f-4e7e6611b10f/linked_in_profile).

## Research Internship / リサーチインターンシップ

We are looking for research interns to join our **Generative Media team** at CyberAgent AI Lab. CyberAgent AI Lab accepts research internship applications year-round from students currently enrolled in a doctoral program. As an intern, you will tackle cutting-edge research together with our research scientists, with the opportunity to publish at top international venues. If you are interested in video and image generation and editing, feel free to get in touch. For details and applications, please see the [research internship page](https://hrmos.co/pages/cyberagent-group/jobs/1936019283746394476583390).

CyberAgent AI Lab の **Generative Media チーム**では、リサーチインターンの学生を募集しています。CyberAgent AI Lab では、博士後期課程に在学中の学生を対象に、リサーチインターンシップの応募を通年で受け付けています。現役のリサーチサイエンティストとともに最先端の研究に取り組み、国際トップカンファレンスへの論文投稿・採択を目指すことができます。動画像の生成・編集に興味のある方は、ぜひお気軽にご連絡ください。詳細・応募については[リサーチインターンシップのページ](https://hrmos.co/pages/cyberagent-group/jobs/1936019283746394476583390)をご覧ください。
